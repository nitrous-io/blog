#### Getting Started is Easy With Nitrous.IO
You might already know that Nitrous.IO is the fastest way to setup a clean new development environment for each of your projects.
But, did you know that once you're ready to *Ship* your code, you can [seamlessly deploy from Nitrous.IO into production on Google App Engine.](http://help.nitrous.io/app-engine/?utm_source=nitrous.io&utm_medium=blog&utm_content=gcpsp) 

#### Google Could Platform Starter Pack
The team at Google wants to make it even sweeter for you to launch your projects into production on their PAAS and IAAS with the [Google Cloud Platform Starter Pack](http://cloud.google.com/starterpack?utm_source=nitrous.io&utm_medium=blog) offer. 
This offer includes $1,000 credit for Google App Engine credit and $1,000 credit for Google Compute Engine. App Engine is a full development stack (PaaS), and Compute Engine lets you run workloads on Linux virtual machines (IaaS). 

**How to Apply** 

For a limited time, Nitrous.IO users can apply for $2,000 of credit to get started building your web or mobile app. 

To apply, follow the steps below:

1. Go to [http://cloud.google.com/starterpack](http://cloud.google.com/starterpack?utm_source=nitrous.io&utm_medium=blog)
2. Click Apply Now
3. Complete the form with this promo code: **NIO-org**
