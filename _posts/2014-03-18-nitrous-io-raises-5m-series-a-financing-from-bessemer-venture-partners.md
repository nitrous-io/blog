### A Brief History

In the past few years, most of the software we use everyday has moved to the cloud.  Email, File Storage, Video Services, Music -- almost everything is a cloud-service these days.  Yet the very people responsible for building our favorite software applications are still downloading and configuring their programming languages, databases and utilities on their own machines.  This process of developing software “locally” is error-prone, repetitive, and incredibly slow. 

<!--break-->

So, in 2012 we set out to build Nitrous.IO with the mission to accelerate software development by making development environments easier to create, configure, and share. 

### Massive adoption

The initial response from the development community has been astounding. In just 7 months since launching our public beta, Nitrous.IO has grown to over 100,000 registered developers from over 4,000 engineering organizations through word-of-mouth alone -- without a dollar spent on marketing!

Developers at companies like Heroku, Hashrocket, and Balanced Payments use Nitrous.IO to snapshot development environments to share with their teams, and to collaborate on code in real time.  Developers have edited code on over 3 million files on the platform and have saved over 500,000 hours in setup and configuration costs.  Professors at major universities like UC Berkeley and NYU are using the platform to provide standard box templates for their students to build applications.  

### Bessemer Venture Partners

With all this momentum, we're excited to announce that we recently closed a $6.5M Series A financing led by [Bessemer Venture Partners](http://www.bvp.com) with participation from CrunchFund and 500 Startups.  Sunil Nagaraj and David Cowan at Bessemer have been our biggest supporters since day one.  They are 100% convinced that the majority of software development will move to the cloud, and are supremely confident that the Nitrous.IO team is best suited to achieve that vision.  

### What we’ll do with the money 

We've achieved so much this past year with a small team, but we have even bigger plans this year with our Web IDE, [desktop apps](https://www.nitrous.io/desktop), [Chrome App](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/nitrousio/efdcneeepllhjlbejkfnaolelbpdacai?hl=en), and team tools.  

With this funding we are aggressively [hiring platform and full stack engineers](https://www.nitrous.io/jobs#platform), [developer evangelists](https://www.nitrous.io/jobs#evangelist) and will continue to build our [product design team](https://www.nitrous.io/jobs#pdesign).  We have some big product announcements coming in April and May, so stay tuned to [@nitrousio](https://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) for updates.  And of course we’ll be printing lots of stickers and t-shirts.  Thousands of them...

### The future of Nitrous.IO

With software development, it's not a question of "if" the majority of applications will be built in the cloud, but "when".  With this funding, we’ve placed ourselves in a position to lead what we believe will be a massive movement of the software application development to the cloud.  The end result will be incredible improvements in the speed of development, collaborative capabilities, and a significant reduction of costs for engineering organizations small and large.

Stay tuned to [@nitrousio](https://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) for updates.  We've got so many great things in store for 2014.
